App sends machineGlass S1 correctly:
  - Full machine identity + org.nr + insurance
  - reporter_role=forare (no swedish_driver_license — by design v1)
  - safety_procedures_followed=ja
  - Glass step2: damage_cause, damaged_location, damage_type, visibility, work_state, surface
  - damage_description (NOT event_narrative)
  - step3: has_counterparty/witness/injuries/third_party/police=false, drivable=true
  - signature in fields; no images in S1 baseline

PDF OK (Glas template):
  - Title: Skadeanmälan - Maskin · Glas- och plåtskador
  - Full Egen maskin block
  - Kompletterande: reporter role, ownership, safety, ALL glass-specific fields
  - Skadebeskrivning shows damage_description
  - Step3 Ja/Nej blocks (motpart, vittnen, personskador, egendom, polis, körbar)
  - 2 pages only

Issues in PDF:
  1. Händelseförloppet (top) = N/A — should show damage_description OR hide section
  2. Artifact: Svenskt körkort / Födelsedatum (not used on machineGlass v1)
  3. Layout: duplicate "Ja X Nej" summary rows at top (polis, personskador, körkort)
  4. Layout: "X" marker on Försäkrad and Körbar booleans
  5. signature image missing on page 2 (labels only)
